# YAQoL - Yet Another Quality of Life Addon

[![Version](https://img.shields.io/badge/version-2.0.16-blue.svg)](https://github.com/Kontaktspray/WOWForeverAddon/releases)
[![WoW Version](https://img.shields.io/badge/WoW-Classic%20%7C%20Anniversary%20%7C%20Mainline-orange.svg)](https://github.com/Kontaktspray/WOWForeverAddon)
[![License](https://img.shields.io/badge/license-MIT-green.svg)](LICENSE)

**YAQoL (Yet Another Quality of Life Addon)** is a lightweight, all-in-one quality-of-life suite for World of Warcraft (Classic Era, Hardcore, Anniversary & Retail). Designed to eliminate tedious manual tasks, streamline chat, optimize camera controls, and highlight quest objectives directly on enemy nameplates, YAQoL improves your daily gameplay without clogging your UI.

---

## ✨ Features Overview

### 📜 Auto Quest Automator
- **Smart Quest Automation**: Automatically accepts available quests and completes quest turn-ins.
- **Item Choice Safety**: Automatically stops before turning in quests that offer multiple equipment choices, allowing you to choose your reward manually.
- **Bypass Key**: Hold **SHIFT** while interacting with NPCs to temporarily bypass auto-questing.

### 💰 Vendor Automations
- **Auto-Repair Gear**: Automatically repairs all damaged equipment upon opening a merchant window and prints the total cost to chat.
- **Auto-Sell Junk**: Instantly sells poor/grey quality items upon opening vendor interfaces, summarizing total gold earned.

### ⚡ General Quality of Life
- **Fast Speed Looting**: Instantly loots all items from targets in a single tick, bypassing Blizzard's slow loot animation delays.
- **Max Camera Distance**: Automatically sets max camera zoom out distance for maximum battlefield visibility.
- **Hide Red Error Messages**: Filters out spammy combat error text (e.g., *"Ability is not ready yet"*, *"Not enough energy"*, *"Out of range"*) while preserving critical quest and system notifications.
- **Auto-Dismount on Action**: Automatically dismounts your character when attempting to cast spells or perform combat actions while mounted.

### 💬 Chat Enhancements
- **Clickable Chat URLs**: Detects web and Discord URLs in chat, making them clickable with a quick copy-to-clipboard dialog.
- **Class-Colored Names**: Colors player names across all chat channels according to their class color.
- **Short Channel Tags**: Replaces long default channel tags with clean abbreviations (e.g., `[1. General]` → `[1]`, `[Guild]` → `[G]`).

### 🎯 Quest Mob Nameplate Indicators
- **Golden Quest Icon & Progress**: Displays a golden quest mark icon and real-time objective progress (e.g., `[2/8]`) directly above the nameplates of quest-required enemies.

### 🔔 Buff & Pet Reminders
- **Class Buff Reminders**: Displays a customizable alert icon when essential class auras or buffs (e.g., Arcane Intellect, Power Word: Fortitude, Mark of the Wild, Demon Armor, Shaman Shields) are missing.
- **Pet Reminders**: Warns Hunter and Warlock players when their active pet is missing before entering combat.

## ⚙️ Configuration & GUI

Access the in-game settings panel anytime with:
```
/yaqol
```
or
```
/yq
```

Features include:
- **Searchable Options System**: Quickly find any setting, toggle, or keyword.
- **Minimap Quick-Access Icon**: Left-click to open options, right-click for quick toggle options.
- **Built-in Changelog Viewer**: View full version histories and updates directly inside the addon.
- **Automatic Version Updates**: Displays a clean update summary modal upon logging in after updating to a new version.

## 📥 Installation

1. Download the latest release `.zip` from [GitHub Releases](https://github.com/Kontaktspray/WOWForeverAddon/releases) or install directly via **Wago.io / WoWUp / CurseForge**.
2. Extract the folder into your World of Warcraft directory:
   `World of Warcraft\_retail_\Interface\AddOns\` (or `_classic_`)
3. Ensure the folder name inside `AddOns` is `WOWForeverAddon` or `YAQoL`.
4. Restart your game or type `/reload` in chat.
